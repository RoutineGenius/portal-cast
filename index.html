📺 Second Screen

Put a window from this computer onto your wall display and keep working here. Enter the 6-digit code shown on the display, then choose what to share.

In the share dialog, click the Window tab and pick one window — your meeting, a document — not Entire Screen. That window appears on the display while your laptop screen stays yours, so it works like a separate screen for that app. Keep the window open (don’t minimize it). Video goes straight from this computer to the display over your network; nothing is stored or uploaded. Same Wi-Fi works best; a VPN may block it.
